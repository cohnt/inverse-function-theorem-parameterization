## Figures

Figures under `static/images/` are derived from the paper source and the
authors' own experiment repositories. Some renders in the source material use
third-party robot meshes (KUKA iiwa, Universal Robots UR5e, Rainbow Robotics
RB-Y1, Schunk WSG gripper) and BlenderKit materials; see the corresponding
`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` in each experiment repository before redistributing
the original full-resolution renders.
